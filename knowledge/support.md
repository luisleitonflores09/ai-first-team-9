# Northstar Labs support

Northstar Labs is a fictional company used for a training demo.

## Getting help

For an account access problem, submit an access support ticket through the fictional Service Desk portal. For a question about onboarding, contact the onboarding buddy.

## Service hours

The fictional Service Desk handles tickets Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. local time. This document does not promise a response time.
