# Northstar Labs equipment

Northstar Labs is a fictional company used for a training demo.

## Requesting equipment

Employees request a laptop or approved accessory through the fictional Service Desk portal. A manager approves each request before the Service Desk prepares the equipment.

## Problems with equipment

For a damaged or malfunctioning laptop, submit an equipment support ticket to the Service Desk and describe the problem. Do not include passwords in the ticket.
