# Northstar Labs onboarding

Northstar Labs is a fictional company used for a training demo.

## First day

New employees meet their manager at 9:00 a.m. on their first working day. The manager shares the employee handbook and introduces the employee to their onboarding buddy.

## First week

During the first week, the onboarding buddy helps the employee request access to the tools listed in the team checklist. The employee completes the security introduction by Friday.
